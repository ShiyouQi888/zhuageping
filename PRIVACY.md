# Privacy Policy for Zhuageping

**Effective date: October 6, 2026**

Zhuageping is a Windows screenshot and screen-recording tool developed by Qi Shiyou. This policy explains how the application processes information. Contact: blacklaw@foxmail.com.

## Information we process

To provide capture, recording, pinning, OCR, and watermark features, Zhuageping processes on your device the screen content you choose, screenshots, recordings, optional microphone audio, and the location, project, notes, and preferences you enter. This content may contain personal information depending on what you choose to capture or enter.

## Use, storage, and security

This information is used only to provide the app's features. Screenshots and recordings are stored in the local output folders that you choose. Settings, history, diagnostics, and backups are stored in the app's local data folder on your device. Zhuageping does not create user accounts, provide cloud sync, serve ads, or sell, rent, or trade your information.

The app relies on your Windows account, device security settings, and chosen storage location to protect local files. You are responsible for securing your device and any files you share.

## Network connections and third parties

The OCR engine runs locally and does not upload images or recognized text. Zhuageping connects to GitHub Releases or GitHub only when you check for updates, download an update, or open this online policy. GitHub may process network-request information, such as an IP address, under its own policies. Zhuageping contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party data-broker service.

## Your choices and controls

You can change screenshot and recording locations, turn off microphone recording, clear history, and use File Explorer to view, copy, or delete saved screenshots, recordings, configuration, diagnostics, and backups. Uninstalling Zhuageping does not automatically remove local files; delete them before or after uninstalling if desired.

## Sharing and other people's information

Zhuageping does not proactively publish your content to external services. If you copy, save, or share screenshots or recordings yourself, the recipient and service's own policies apply. Please confirm that you have the necessary authorization before capturing content that contains another person's personal information.

## Children and policy changes

Zhuageping is not directed to children and does not intentionally collect children's information. We may update this policy when functionality changes and will update the effective date in the app and on this page. For privacy questions or access or deletion requests, contact blacklaw@foxmail.com.
